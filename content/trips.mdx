### Kuala Rompin, Pahang Full-Day Charter

- **Category:** Saltwater
- **Group:** Expedition
- **Summary:** Our flagship full-day charter out of Kuala Rompin, Pahang, built around classic live-bait sea fishing rather than jigging or popping. The day is spent bottom fishing and live-bait drifting over Rompin's reef structures and current lines, with a shot at a wide spread of species — Pacific sailfish, Spanish barred mackerel, cobia, snappers, groupers, great barracuda, yellow tail barracuda, and queenfish. Covers a full day on the water (roughly 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM), with boat charter, guide, fishing gear, drinks, and lunch packs included for up to 4 anglers, with room for up to 6. Ideal for anglers who want relaxed, bait-based fishing without needing prior jigging experience.

- **Description:** This is our flagship trip and the one we run most often out of Kuala Rompin, Pahang — a full day of straightforward, bait-based sea fishing rather than the more technical jigging or popping styles offered elsewhere.

The day is split between two techniques: bottom fishing and live-bait drifting. Bottom fishing has us holding position over known reef structure, sending live bait down for snappers and groupers. Live-bait drift moves with the current to work more mobile predators — Spanish barred mackerel, great barracuda, yellow tail barracuda, queenfish, and on the right day, cobia and Pacific sailfish. Your guide calls the technique and spot based on tide, current, and recent bites.

Kuala Rompin's waters offer varied light-to-medium tackle sea fishing. Sailfish and cobia are the headline draws, but snappers and groupers form a reliable backbone of most trips, with barracuda showing up often enough to be routine.

A typical day departs the jetty around 8:00 AM, breaks for lunch on board, and returns around 5:30 PM. No jigging or popping gear is used — all live-bait tackle is provided and set up by the guide, making this suited to newer anglers as well as experienced ones who prefer bait over lures.

Included in the RM3,800 flat rate: boat charter, guide, fishing gear, drinks, and lunch packs, covering up to 4 anglers. Up to 2 additional anglers can join at RM150 per pax, for a maximum of 6.
- **Trip Type:** Day Trip (no overnight stay)
- **Duration:** Full Day, ~9.5 hours (depart jetty ~8:00 AM, return ~5:30 PM)
- **Location:** Kuala Rompin, Pahang
- **Coordinates:** 2.80, 103.50
- **Price:** RM3,800 — per_trip (flat rate, inclusive of boat charter, canned drinks & drinking water, lunch packs, guide, and fishing gear — covers up to 4 pax)
- **Max Guests:** 6
- **Season:** Open season March – mid-October. Best time to book: March–May (open fishing season) and Aug–Oct (sailfish season).
- **Add-ons:**
  - Additional angler (5th–6th pax, beyond the 4 included) — RM150 per pax

### Tioman Island, Pahang Expedition

- **Category:** Saltwater
- **Group:** Expedition
- **Summary:** Our multi-day expedition to Tioman Island, Pahang, departing from Kuala Rompin — a longer-format version of our flagship live-bait sea fishing, run over a minimum of 3 days and up to 5. Fishing follows the same bottom fishing and live-bait drift techniques as the Rompin trip, with an expanded target list adding dorado, red snapper, emperor, wahoo, and black marlin to the usual sailfish, mackerel, cobia, snapper, grouper, barracuda, and queenfish. Rate is RM4,500 per day, inclusive of daily drinks and lunch, guide, and fishing gear, for up to 4 anglers (up to 6 with the extra-pax add-on). Accommodation on Tioman is optional at RM350 per night for 2 pax, or anglers may arrange their own.

- **Description:** This is our expedition-format trip for anglers who want more time on the water than a single day allows. It departs from Kuala Rompin and runs out to Tioman Island, Pahang, with a minimum booking of 3 days' fishing and the option to extend up to 5.

Fishing technique and structure carry over directly from our Kuala Rompin trip — bottom fishing over reef structure for snappers and groupers, and live-bait drift for the more mobile predators. The extended range and time at Tioman opens up a wider target list: alongside the usual Pacific sailfish, Spanish barred mackerel, cobia, snappers, groupers, barracuda, and queenfish, anglers here also have a shot at dorado, red snapper, emperor, wahoo, and black marlin.

As with Rompin, no jigging or popping is involved — this is a live-bait trip throughout, with all tackle provided and set up by the guide.

Accommodation is separate from the fishing rate: a standard 2-night stay is available at RM350 for 2 pax per night, or anglers are welcome to source their own lodging on the island if preferred.

The RM4,500 per-day rate covers boat charter, guide, fishing gear, daily drinks, and lunch, for up to 4 anglers, with an RM200-per-pax add-on for up to 2 additional anglers (max 6).

### Royal Belum State Park, Perak Expedition

- **Category:** Freshwater
- **Group:** Expedition
- **Summary:** A freshwater fishing expedition into Royal Belum State Park, Perak, run over a minimum of 2D1N and departing by boat from Pulau Banding into Royal Belum or Temenggor Lake. The focus here shifts from our saltwater trips to freshwater sport fishing, targeting giant snakehead (toman) and sebarau (hampala barb) across the lake's flooded forest and river systems. Rate is RM1,700 per day, inclusive of boat, guide, canned drinks and drinking water, and lunch — with each boat capped at 2 anglers, so larger groups will need multiple boats. Fishing runs 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM daily; anglers bring their own casting gear, as fishing equipment is not included.

- **Description:** Royal Belum State Park, together with the adjoining Temenggor Lake, is one of the largest freshwater fishing grounds in Peninsular Malaysia, and this expedition is our dedicated freshwater offering, distinct from the saltwater trips out of Kuala Rompin and Tioman. It's built around a minimum stay of 2D1N, with the option to extend for a longer expedition.

Each fishing day runs from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with boats departing from Pulau Banding into the flooded forest and river systems of Royal Belum or across the open water of Temenggor Lake, depending on conditions and where the guide expects the best action.

The two headline species here are giant snakehead (toman) and sebarau (hampala barb) — both aggressive, structure-oriented predators that respond well to casting and lure techniques around submerged trees, drop-offs, and river mouths feeding into the lake. Toman in particular are known for explosive strikes and hard fights, making this a favorite for anglers looking for something different from typical bottom or drift saltwater fishing.

Boats on this trip are capped at 2 anglers each, so groups larger than 2 will need to book multiple boats to fish together. Unlike our saltwater trips, fishing equipment is not included here — anglers should bring their own full range of gear for freshwater casting.

The RM1,700-per-day rate covers the boat, guide, canned drinks and drinking water, and lunch. Given the remote lake setting, this runs as a self-contained package with meals and drinks provided throughout the stay.

- **Trip Type:** Overnight / Expedition (freshwater fishing)
- **Duration:** Minimum 2D1N (2 days, 1 night); fishing hours 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily
- **Location:** Royal Belum State Park, Perak — sessions start 8:00 AM from Pulau Banding, heading to Royal Belum or Temenggor Lake
- **Coordinates:** 5.55, 101.37
- **Price:** RM1,700 per day — per_trip (per boat), inclusive of boat, guide, canned drinks & drinking water, and lunch. Fishing equipment NOT included — anglers bring their own full range of gear for freshwater casting.
- **Max Guests:** 2 anglers per boat (additional boats required for larger groups)
- **Season:** Sebarau (hampala barb) bite best Jan–Apr; giant snakehead (toman) bite best Aug–Oct. Nov–Dec is the rainy season and affects both species.

### Natural Exotic Fishing Pond, Perak

- **Category:** Pond
- **Group:** Recreational
- **Summary:** A different kind of trip from our boat charters — a day session at a man-made fishing pond in Perak stocked with an unusual mix of large exotic freshwater species not found in local wild waters, including arapaima, alligator gar, Mekong catfish, chao phraya catfish, amazon and Asian redtail catfish, pacu, sultan fish (mad barb), wallago, and giant snakehead. Great for anglers who want a shot at species they'd otherwise never encounter in Malaysia. Rate is RM350 per pax, inclusive of gear, guide, drinking water, and canned drinks.

- **Description:** This trip is a departure from our river, sea, and lake charters — instead of a boat, this is a day of fishing at a stocked, man-made pond in Perak built specifically to give anglers a shot at large exotic species that don't naturally occur in Malaysian waters.

The pond is stocked with a genuinely unusual species list, including arapaima, alligator gar, giant Mekong catfish, chao phraya catfish, amazon redtail catfish, Asian redtail catfish, pacu, sultan fish (also known as mad barb), wallago, and giant snakehead, among others. Many of these fish grow to serious sizes, and the appeal is largely the novelty and raw pulling power of species anglers won't find in local rivers, lakes, or the sea.

Both bait and lure fishing are generally productive here, and it's a good option for anglers of any experience level, including those newer to fishing, since gear and guiding are provided.

The RM350 per pax rate includes fishing gear, a guide, drinking water, and canned drinks, so anglers can turn up without needing to bring their own equipment.

### Fish Valley, Semenyih

- **Category:** Pond
- **Group:** Recreational
- **Summary:** A relaxed, beginner-friendly pond fishing trip at Fish Valley in Semenyih, Selangor — a popular family pay pond stocked with pacu, Mekong catfish, tilapia, java barb (lampam), several carp varieties (common, grass, bighead, rohu), patin, and sultan fish. Easygoing fishing that suits first-timers and kids as well as more experienced anglers chasing a big pacu. Rate is RM150 per pax, inclusive, with two session options: 8:00 AM–12:00 PM or 12:00 PM–6:00 PM.

- **Description:** Fish Valley in Semenyih is one of the better-known family pay ponds in Selangor, and this trip is our beginner-friendly option for anglers who want an easy, low-pressure day of fishing without needing prior experience.

The pond is stocked with a friendly mix of species that keeps things interesting for anglers of any skill level — pacu (the pond's signature hard-fighting species), Mekong catfish, tilapia, java barb (lampam), several carp varieties including common carp, grass carp, bighead carp, and rohu (Indian carp), along with patin and sultan fish turning up from time to time. Bites tend to come often enough to keep beginners and kids engaged, while a big pacu or catfish can still give more experienced anglers a proper fight.

Two session slots are available: a morning session from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM, or an afternoon session from 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM. The RM150 per pax rate is inclusive, making this a simple, low-commitment option to add alongside our more technical saltwater and freshwater expedition trips.

- **Trip Type:** Day Trip (no overnight stay)
